📌 **Gap Analysis: Finding the Difference Between Where We Are and Where We Want to Be**

Imagine you are standing at **Point A** and want to reach **Point B**. 🗺️

You know:

📍 Where you are now
🎯 Where you want to go

The distance between these two points is the **gap**.

In business and projects, **Gap Analysis** helps us identify the difference between our **current state** and our **desired future state**.

### 📍 Current State

This means:

👉 Where are we now?

For example:

* Our sales are 700 products per month
* Customer support takes 5 days
* We have 2 developers
* Our website has many technical problems

### 🎯 Desired State

This means:

👉 Where do we want to be?

For example:

* We want to sell 1,000 products per month
* Customer support should solve problems within 1 day
* We need 5 developers
* We want a faster and better website

### 🔍 The Gap

Now we compare the current state with the desired state.

For example:

📊 Current sales: 700 products
🎯 Desired sales: 1,000 products

👉 **Gap: 300 products**

The next question is:

❓ **Why does this gap exist?**

Maybe:

* The product is not well known
* Marketing is weak
* The price is too high
* Customers are choosing competitors

This is where Gap Analysis becomes useful.

---

## 📌 Common Types of Gaps

### 1️⃣ Performance Gap

The difference between actual performance and expected performance.

Example:

🎯 Expected sales: 1,000 products
📊 Actual sales: 700 products

👉 The difference is a **Performance Gap**.

---

### 2️⃣ Skill Gap

The difference between the skills people currently have and the skills they need.

Example:

👨‍💼 Current skill: Basic Excel
🎯 Required skill: Advanced Excel and Data Analysis

👉 The difference is a **Skill Gap**.

---

### 3️⃣ Process Gap

The difference between the current process and the desired process.

Example:

📦 Current delivery process: 5 days
🎯 Desired delivery process: 2 days

👉 The business needs to find out why the process is slow and how to improve it.

---

### 4️⃣ Technology Gap

The difference between the technology a business currently uses and the technology it needs.

Example:

📄 Current: Manual order tracking
💻 Desired: Automated order tracking system

👉 The difference is a **Technology Gap**.

---

### 5️⃣ Resource Gap

A business may not have enough resources to achieve its goals.

Resources can include:

👥 People
💰 Money
⏰ Time
🛠️ Equipment

Example:

A project needs 5 developers, but the company has only 2.

👉 This is a **Resource Gap**.

---

### 6️⃣ Knowledge Gap

The difference between what people currently know and what they need to know.

Example:

A company wants to use AI, but the team does not know how to use AI technology.

👉 This is a **Knowledge Gap**.

---

### 7️⃣ Compliance Gap

The difference between current business practices and required laws, rules, or regulations.

Example:

A company collects customer information but does not follow required data protection rules.

👉 This is a **Compliance Gap**.

---

## 📌 How to Perform a Gap Analysis

A simple Gap Analysis usually follows these steps:

### 1️⃣ Understand the Current State

Ask:

👉 Where are we now?

Collect information about the current situation.

### 2️⃣ Define the Desired State

Ask:

👉 Where do we want to be?

Set a clear goal.

### 3️⃣ Identify the Gap

Compare the current state with the desired state.

👉 What is missing?

### 4️⃣ Find the Root Cause

Ask:

👉 Why does this gap exist?

Finding the cause is important because solving the wrong problem can waste time and money.

### 5️⃣ Create an Action Plan

Decide:

✅ What should we do?
✅ Who will do it?
✅ What resources are needed?
✅ When should it be completed?

### 6️⃣ Measure the Progress

After taking action, check:

👉 Did the gap become smaller?

If not, the organization may need to change its approach.

---

## 💡 A Simple Business Example

Imagine an online store wants to increase monthly sales.

📍 **Current State:** 700 orders per month
🎯 **Desired State:** 1,000 orders per month
🔍 **Gap:** 300 orders

After analysis, the business discovers:

* The website is slow
* Product information is incomplete
* The checkout process is complicated

Now the business can create a plan:

✅ Improve website speed
✅ Add better product information
✅ Simplify the checkout process

This is how Gap Analysis helps a business move from its current situation toward its goal.

📌 **In simple words:**

**Gap Analysis means understanding:**

📍 Where are we now?
🎯 Where do we want to be?
🔍 What is missing?
❓ Why is it missing?
🛠️ What should we do to close the gap?

As a Business Analyst, Gap Analysis is useful for identifying problems, improving processes, finding missing capabilities, and helping organizations achieve their business goals.

👉 **Find the gap. Understand the reason. Create a plan. Close the gap.**

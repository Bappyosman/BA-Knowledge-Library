## 📌 SWOT Analysis: A Simple Way to Understand a Business

Before starting a business or project, we need to understand its current situation.

👉 **What are we good at?**
👉 **Where do we have problems?**
👉 **What opportunities can we use?**
👉 **What risks can affect us?**

**SWOT Analysis** helps us answer these questions.

### 🔹 S = Strengths

Things we are good at.

Example:

* Strong brand
* Skilled team
* Good product quality
* Loyal customers

### 🔹 W = Weaknesses

Things that create problems or need improvement.

Example:

* Limited budget
* Small team
* Lack of experience
* Poor customer service

### 🔹 O = Opportunities

External situations that can help the business grow.

Example:

* Growing market demand
* New technology
* New customer groups
* Weak competitors

### 🔹 T = Threats

External risks that can create problems.

Example:

* Strong competitors
* Changing customer needs
* New government regulations
* Rising costs

### 💡 Simple Example: A Small Coffee Shop

**Strengths:** Good coffee and friendly service ☕
**Weaknesses:** Small space and limited budget 💰
**Opportunities:** More people are ordering food online 📱
**Threats:** A large coffee chain opens nearby 🏪

📌 **In simple words:**

**SWOT Analysis helps us understand where we are, what we can improve, what opportunities we can use, and what risks we should prepare for.**

As a Business Analyst, SWOT Analysis can help organizations make better decisions before starting a project, launching a product, or creating a business strategy.

👉 **Before making a decision, understand both your strengths and your risks.**

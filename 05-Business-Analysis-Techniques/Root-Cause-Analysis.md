## 📌 Root Cause Analysis (RCA): Finding the Real Reason Behind a Problem

When something goes wrong, many people only try to fix the visible problem.

But a Business Analyst asks:

👉 **"Why did this problem happen in the first place?"**

This is called **Root Cause Analysis**, or **RCA**.

### 💡 Simple Example

Imagine an online store's website is not working.

❌ **Problem:** Customers cannot place orders.

A quick solution might be:

🔧 Restart the server.

The website works again.

But if the same problem happens every day, the real problem has not been solved.

So we ask:

❓ Why can customers not place orders?

Maybe:

1️⃣ The payment system is failing.
2️⃣ Why is the payment system failing?
👉 The payment server is overloaded.

3️⃣ Why is the server overloaded?
👉 The server cannot handle many users.

4️⃣ Why can the server not handle many users?
👉 The system was not designed for the expected number of users.

🎯 **Root Cause:** The system infrastructure is not capable of handling the required traffic.

Now we can solve the real problem instead of repeatedly restarting the server.

## 🔍 The 5 Whys Technique

One of the most popular Root Cause Analysis techniques is the **5 Whys**.

You simply keep asking **"Why?"** until you find the underlying cause.

### Example:

❌ **Problem:** A customer received the wrong product.

**Why 1:** Why did the customer receive the wrong product?
👉 The warehouse worker selected the wrong item.

**Why 2:** Why did the worker select the wrong item?
👉 Similar products were stored next to each other.

**Why 3:** Why were similar products stored together?
👉 The warehouse had no proper product organization system.

**Why 4:** Why was there no proper organization system?
👉 The company never created a standard storage process.

🎯 **Root Cause:** No standardized warehouse organization process.

The solution is not simply to tell the employee:

❌ "Be more careful."

A better solution may be:

✅ Create a proper storage system
✅ Use product codes or barcodes
✅ Create a standard operating procedure

## 📌 Common Root Cause Analysis Tools

### 🔹 1. 5 Whys

Keep asking "Why?" to find the deeper cause.

### 🔹 2. Fishbone Diagram

Also called an **Ishikawa Diagram**.

It helps organize possible causes into categories such as:

👥 People
⚙️ Process
💻 Technology
📦 Materials
🏢 Environment
📏 Measurement

### 🔹 3. Pareto Analysis

The **80/20 rule** helps identify the small number of causes responsible for most problems.

For example:

👉 20% of problems may cause 80% of customer complaints.

## 📌 Why is Root Cause Analysis Important?

RCA helps organizations:

✅ Solve the real problem
✅ Prevent the problem from happening again
✅ Reduce repeated costs
✅ Improve business processes
✅ Improve product quality
✅ Make better decisions

📌 **In simple words:**

**Don't just fix what happened. Find out why it happened.**

A good Business Analyst does not only ask:

❌ **"What is the problem?"**

They also ask:

👉 **"Why did the problem happen?"**

And most importantly:

👉 **"How can we prevent it from happening again?"**

📌 **Feasibility Study: Should We Start This Business?**

Imagine you have a business idea.

Before spending money and starting the business, you should ask:

👉 **"Can this business really work?"**

A **Feasibility Study** helps answer this question.

It is like checking the road before starting a journey. 🛣️

### 🔹 1. Market Feasibility

Do people actually want this product or service?

Example:

If you want to sell umbrellas, you should first check:

☔ Do people need umbrellas?
☔ Who will buy them?
☔ Who are the competitors?

### 🔹 2. Technical Feasibility

Can we actually build or operate the business?

For example:

💻 Do we have the technology?
👨‍💻 Do we have the right people?
🛠️ Do we have the necessary tools?

### 🔹 3. Financial Feasibility

Can we afford to start and run the business?

We need to think about:

💰 How much money is needed?
💰 How much money can we earn?
💰 Can the business make a profit?

### 🔹 4. Operational Feasibility

Can the business work properly every day?

For example:

📦 Can we deliver products?
👥 Can we manage customers?
🏪 Can we run the daily operations?

### 🔹 5. Legal Feasibility

Is the business allowed by law?

We need to check:

⚖️ Licenses
⚖️ Business rules
⚖️ Government regulations

### 💡 Simple Example

Imagine you want to open a small ice cream shop.

Before starting, you should ask:

✅ Do people in this area like ice cream?
✅ Are there already many ice cream shops?
✅ Can I afford the shop and equipment?
✅ Can I get the required license?
✅ Can I manage the shop every day?

If the answers are mostly positive, the business may be feasible.

If there are serious problems, you can change your plan before losing money.

📌 **In simple words:**

A Feasibility Study helps us understand:

👉 **"Is this idea possible, practical, and worth doing?"**

As a Business Analyst, conducting a feasibility study before starting a project can help an organization make better decisions and reduce unnecessary risk.

💡 **Think before you build. Check before you invest.**

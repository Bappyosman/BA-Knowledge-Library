# 📊 KPI Definition & Tracking: Understanding If We Are Doing Well

Imagine you are learning to ride a bicycle. 🚲

You want to know:

👉 Am I getting better?
👉 Am I riding faster?
👉 Can I ride farther than before?

You need something to measure your progress.

For example:

📏 Distance ridden
⏱️ Time taken
🚴 Speed

These measurements help you understand your progress.

Businesses do the same thing.

They use **KPIs**.

---

# 🔹 What Is a KPI?

**KPI** stands for:

> **Key Performance Indicator**

That sounds complicated, but the idea is simple.

> **A KPI is a measurement that helps us understand whether we are achieving an important goal.**

### Example:

A business wants to get more customers.

Its goal is:

🎯 **Get 1,000 new customers in 3 months.**

A KPI could be:

📊 **Number of new customers**

If the business gets:

* Month 1: 200 customers
* Month 2: 350 customers
* Month 3: 500 customers

The business can track its progress.

---

# 🎯 Goal vs. KPI

These two are different.

### 🎯 Goal

What do we want to achieve?

> “We want to increase sales.”

### 📊 KPI

How do we measure success?

> “Monthly sales revenue.”

So:

**Goal = Destination** 🏁
**KPI = Measurement that shows progress** 📊

# 📌 What Is KPI Definition?

Before tracking a KPI, we need to clearly define it.

A good KPI definition should answer:

1️⃣ What are we measuring?
2️⃣ Why are we measuring it?
3️⃣ How will we calculate it?
4️⃣ What is our target?
5️⃣ How often will we check it?

---

# 📊 Example: Online Marketplace

Imagine we have an online marketplace.

### Business Goal:

🎯 Increase customer satisfaction.

### KPI:

📊 Customer Satisfaction Score

### Definition:

> Measure how satisfied customers are after using the marketplace.

### Target:

🎯 Achieve a satisfaction score of 90%.

### Tracking:

📅 Check every month.

Now the KPI is clearly defined.

# 📌 Important Parts of a KPI

## 1️⃣ KPI Name

What are we measuring?

Example:

📊 **Monthly Revenue**

---

## 2️⃣ Definition

What does the KPI mean?

Example:

> Total money earned from sales in one month.

---

## 3️⃣ Formula

How do we calculate it?

For example:

**Monthly Revenue = Total Sales Amount**

Another example:

**Conversion Rate = Number of Purchases ÷ Number of Visitors × 100**

---

## 4️⃣ Target

What result do we want?

Example:

🎯 Target: **10,000 monthly visitors**

---

## 5️⃣ Time Period

When do we measure it?

Examples:

📅 Daily
📅 Weekly
📅 Monthly
📅 Quarterly

---

## 6️⃣ Responsible Person

Who checks the KPI?

For example:

👨‍💼 Business Analyst
📊 Data Analyst
👩‍💼 Marketing Manager

# 📌 What Is KPI Tracking?

**KPI Tracking** means regularly checking KPI results.

For example:

| Month    |          Target | Actual Result | Status          |
| -------- | --------------: | ------------: | --------------- |
| January  | 1,000 customers |           800 | ⚠️ Below Target |
| February | 1,000 customers |         1,100 | ✅ Achieved      |
| March    | 1,000 customers |         1,300 | 🚀 Above Target |

This helps the business understand its performance.

🔴 **Red** = Serious problem

# 📌 Leading vs. Lagging KPIs

## 🔮 Leading KPI

A measurement that may help predict future results.

Example:

📢 Number of website visitors

If more people visit a website, sales may increase in the future.

---

## 📊 Lagging KPI

A measurement that shows what already happened.

Example:

💰 Monthly Revenue

It tells us how much money the business already earned.

### Simple Difference:

🔮 **Leading KPI = What may happen**
📊 **Lagging KPI = What already happened**

# ⚠️ Not Everything Is a KPI

This is very important.

A business may have hundreds of things it can measure.

But not every measurement is a **Key Performance Indicator**.

For example:

A website may track:

👀 Page views
🖱️ Button clicks
⏱️ Time spent on a page

But the most important KPI may be:

🛒 **Completed Purchases**

Because the main business goal is selling products.

👉 A KPI should connect to an important business goal.

---

### KPI:

# 📌 A Simple KPI Definition Example

### KPI Name:

📦 Order Delivery Time

### What does it measure?

How long it takes to deliver an order.

### Formula:

**Delivery Time = Delivery Date − Order Date**

### Target:

🎯 Deliver orders within 3 days.

### Frequency:

📅 Track every week.

### Responsible Person:

👨‍💼 Operations Manager.

This is a properly defined KPI.

# 🎯 The Main Idea

Imagine you are playing a game. 🎮

You need a score to know:

👉 Are you winning?
👉 Are you losing?
👉 Are you improving?

Businesses use KPIs in a similar way.

> **A KPI is a measurement that shows whether a business, project, team, or process is moving toward an important goal.**

The process is:

🎯 **Set a Goal**
⬇️
📊 **Define a KPI**
⬇️
📈 **Track the KPI**
⬇️
🔍 **Analyze the Result**
⬇️
💡 **Take Action**

**If you cannot measure progress, it becomes difficult to know whether you are truly improving.** 🚀

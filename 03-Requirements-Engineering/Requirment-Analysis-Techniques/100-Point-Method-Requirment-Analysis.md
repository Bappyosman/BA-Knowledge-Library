## 📌 100-Point Method: A Simple Way to Prioritize Requirements

In a project, we often have many requirements, but limited time, budget, and resources.

The problem is:

👉 **Which requirement is more important?**

The **100-Point Method** helps a team prioritize requirements by giving a total of **100 points** to distribute among different features or requirements.

### 💡 How does it work?

Imagine a project has these requirements:

* User Registration
* Product Search
* Online Payment
* Order Tracking
* Product Reviews

The team has a total of **100 points**.

They distribute the points based on the importance of each requirement.

| Requirement       |  Points |
| ----------------- | ------: |
| User Registration |      20 |
| Product Search    |      25 |
| Online Payment    |      30 |
| Order Tracking    |      15 |
| Product Reviews   |      10 |
| **Total**         | **100** |

The requirement with the highest points has the highest priority.

In this example:

🥇 **Online Payment = 30 points**
🥈 **Product Search = 25 points**
🥉 **User Registration = 20 points**

This tells the team which requirements are considered most important.

---

### 📌 Simple Example

Imagine you are building an e-commerce marketplace.

You have **100 points** to spend.

You might decide:

🔴 **Payment System: 30 points**
Because customers cannot complete purchases without it.

🟠 **Product Search: 25 points**
Because users need to find products easily.

🟡 **User Registration: 20 points**
Important for creating user accounts and managing orders.

🔵 **Order Tracking: 15 points**
Useful for customers, but the business can operate without it initially.

⚪ **Product Reviews: 10 points**
Helpful, but not essential for the first version.

The total is:

**30 + 25 + 20 + 15 + 10 = 100 points**

---

## 📌 Why is the 100-Point Method useful?

It helps teams:

✅ Compare different requirements
✅ Identify the most important features
✅ Make prioritization more objective
✅ Involve stakeholders in decision-making
✅ Reduce arguments about which feature comes first
✅ Focus on the features that provide the most value

---

## 💡 The Important Idea

Instead of saying:

> "I think this feature is more important."

A stakeholder can say:

> "I will give this feature 30 points because it has a higher business value."

This makes the discussion more structured.

---

## 📌 Important Point

The 100-Point Method does **not** mean that a feature with 30 points is automatically the first feature to develop.

The team should also consider:

* Business value
* Customer needs
* Cost
* Technical complexity
* Risk
* Dependencies
* Project goals

For example, a feature may receive **30 points** because it is very important, but it may also be extremely expensive or technically difficult to build.

So, the final decision should consider both **priority and feasibility**.

### 👉 In simple words:

**The 100-Point Method helps us answer one important question:**

> **"If we have only 100 points to spend, which requirements deserve the most points?"**

As a Business Analyst, this method can help stakeholders and project teams make better decisions about **what to build first and what to build later**.

# 👥 Stakeholder Analysis & Stakeholder Matrix

Imagine you are building a **food delivery app**. 🍔📱

Many people may be connected to the project:

👤 Customers
👨‍💻 Developers
🏢 Business Owner
🍽️ Restaurant Owners
🚚 Delivery Partners
💳 Payment Providers

But all of them do not have the same level of interest or influence.

So, how do we know:

👉 Who is important?
👉 Who should we talk to regularly?
👉 Who only needs updates?
👉 Who can affect the project?

This is where **Stakeholder Analysis** and the **Stakeholder Matrix** help.

---

# 🔹 What Is a Stakeholder?

A **stakeholder** is a person, group, or organization that:

> **Can affect the project or can be affected by the project.**

### Example: Online Marketplace

👤 Customer → Uses the platform
🏪 Seller → Sells products
👨‍💼 Admin → Manages the system
👨‍💻 Developer → Builds the system
💼 Business Owner → Invests money and makes decisions

All of them are stakeholders.

---

# 🔍 What Is Stakeholder Analysis?

**Stakeholder Analysis** means understanding the people connected to a project.

We try to understand:

👤 Who are they?
🎯 What do they want?
💪 How much power do they have?
❤️ How interested are they?
⚠️ Can they create problems?
🤝 How should we communicate with them?

---

# 🛒 Example: E-Commerce Marketplace

Imagine you are building an online marketplace.

| Stakeholder          | Interest | Power  |
| -------------------- | -------- | ------ |
| Business Owner       | High     | High   |
| Customer             | High     | Low    |
| Developer            | High     | Medium |
| Seller               | High     | Medium |
| Government Authority | Low      | High   |

Now we understand that different stakeholders need different types of communication.

---

# 🧩 What Is a Stakeholder Matrix?

A **Stakeholder Matrix** is a simple tool that helps us categorize stakeholders based on:

📊 **Power**: How much influence do they have?

❤️ **Interest**: How much do they care about the project?

The most common matrix is:

> **Power-Interest Grid**

---

# 📊 The 4 Categories

## 🔴 1. High Power + High Interest

### 👉 Manage Closely

These stakeholders are extremely important.

They:

💪 Have strong influence
❤️ Care deeply about the project

### Examples:

💼 Business Owner
👨‍💼 Project Sponsor
🏢 Senior Manager

### What should you do?

✅ Communicate regularly
✅ Involve them in important decisions
✅ Understand their expectations
✅ Give them regular updates

---

## 🟠 2. High Power + Low Interest

### 👉 Keep Satisfied

These stakeholders have a lot of power but may not be interested in daily project details.

### Examples:

🏢 Company Director
🏛️ Government Authority
💼 Senior Executive

### What should you do?

✅ Give important updates
✅ Do not overload them with small details
✅ Keep them satisfied

---

## 🟡 3. Low Power + High Interest

### 👉 Keep Informed

These stakeholders care a lot about the project but do not have much decision-making power.

### Examples:

👤 Customers
🏪 Sellers
👨‍💻 Team Members

### What should you do?

✅ Give regular updates
✅ Listen to their feedback
✅ Understand their problems
✅ Keep them informed

---

## 🔵 4. Low Power + Low Interest

### 👉 Monitor

These stakeholders have little influence and little interest.

### Examples:

👥 General public
📋 People indirectly connected to the project

### What should you do?

✅ Monitor them
✅ Give information when necessary
❌ Do not spend too much time managing them

---

# 👨‍💼 What Does a Business Analyst Do?

A Business Analyst usually:

1️⃣ Identifies stakeholders
2️⃣ Understands their roles
3️⃣ Understands their needs
4️⃣ Analyzes their power and interest
5️⃣ Places them in the Stakeholder Matrix
6️⃣ Creates a communication plan
7️⃣ Manages their expectations

---

# ⚠️ Why Is Stakeholder Analysis Important?

Imagine you build a product without talking to the important stakeholders.

You may build:

❌ The wrong features
❌ The wrong process
❌ Something users do not need

Stakeholder Analysis helps you understand:

> **Who should be involved before making important decisions?**

---

# 🎯 The Main Idea

Think of a project like a team game. ⚽

Some people:

💰 Provide money
🎯 Make decisions
👥 Use the product
💻 Build the product
📢 Provide feedback

Everyone has a different role.

The **Stakeholder Matrix** helps us understand:

> **Who has power, who has interest, and how we should manage each stakeholder.**

### Simple Formula:

🔍 **Identify Stakeholders**
⬇️
📊 **Analyze Power & Interest**
⬇️
🗂️ **Place Them in the Matrix**
⬇️
💬 **Create a Communication Strategy**
⬇️
🤝 **Manage Relationships**

For a Business Analyst:

> **Understanding people is just as important as understanding technology.** 🚀

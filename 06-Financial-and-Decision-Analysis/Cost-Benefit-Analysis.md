## 📌 Cost-Benefit Analysis: Is It Worth Doing?

Imagine you want to buy a bicycle. 🚲

Before buying it, you may ask:

💰 **How much will it cost?**
🎁 **What benefits will I get?**

If the benefits are greater than the cost, buying the bicycle may be a good decision.

This is the basic idea of **Cost-Benefit Analysis (CBA)**.

### 🔹 What is Cost-Benefit Analysis?

Cost-Benefit Analysis is a method used to compare:

**Total Costs** 💰
with
**Total Benefits** 🎁

It helps us answer:

👉 **"Are the expected benefits worth the costs?"**

---

## 📌 Simple Business Example

Imagine a company wants to develop a new mobile application.

### 💰 Costs

* App development: $10,000
* Marketing: $3,000
* Maintenance: $2,000

**Total Cost = $15,000**

### 🎁 Benefits

* Expected additional revenue: $25,000
* Reduced manual work: $5,000

**Total Benefit = $30,000**

### 📊 Comparison

**Benefits = $30,000**
**Costs = $15,000**

👉 The benefits are greater than the costs.

Therefore, the project may be financially beneficial.

---

## 📌 Types of Costs

### 💰 Direct Costs

Costs directly related to the project.

Example:

* Developer salary
* Software
* Equipment

### ⏰ Indirect Costs

Costs that may not be directly connected to the project.

Example:

* Employee training time
* Office resources
* Management time

### 🔄 Ongoing Costs

Costs that continue after the project is launched.

Example:

* Maintenance
* Hosting
* Customer support

---

## 📌 Types of Benefits

### 💵 Financial Benefits

Benefits that directly generate or save money.

Example:

* Increased sales
* Reduced operating costs
* Higher profit

### 😊 Non-Financial Benefits

Benefits that may not be easily measured in money.

Example:

* Better customer satisfaction
* Improved brand reputation
* Happier employees
* Better user experience

---

## 📌 How to Perform a Cost-Benefit Analysis

1️⃣ **Identify all costs**
How much money, time, and resources are needed?

2️⃣ **Identify all benefits**
What value will the project provide?

3️⃣ **Estimate the value**
Try to measure the costs and benefits.

4️⃣ **Compare them**
Are the benefits greater than the costs?

5️⃣ **Make a decision**
Should the project continue, change, or stop?

---

## 💡 In Simple Words

Before starting a project, ask:

> **"What will we spend?"** 💰

and

> **"What will we get in return?"** 🎁

If the expected benefits are much greater than the costs, the project may be a good investment.

As a Business Analyst, Cost-Benefit Analysis helps organizations make better decisions before investing time, money, and resources in a project.

👉 **Don't just ask, "Can we build it?"**

Also ask:

**"Is it worth building?"**

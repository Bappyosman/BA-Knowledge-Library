## 📌 ROI Analysis: Is the Investment Worth It?

Imagine you spend **৳10,000** on a business idea.

After some time, you earn **৳15,000** from that investment.

Now you may ask:

👉 **How much profit did I make compared to the money I invested?**

This is what **ROI Analysis** helps us understand.

### 🔹 What is ROI?

**ROI** means **Return on Investment**.

It measures how much return or profit you receive from an investment compared to how much you invested.

### 📊 Simple Formula

**ROI = (Net Profit ÷ Investment Cost) × 100**

### 💡 Example

You invest:

💰 Investment = ৳10,000

You receive:

💵 Return = ৳15,000

Your profit is:

**৳15,000 − ৳10,000 = ৳5,000**

So:

**ROI = (৳5,000 ÷ ৳10,000) × 100 = 50%**

👉 Your ROI is **50%**.

This means you earned a **50% return** on your investment.

---

## 📌 Business Example

Imagine a company spends **৳50,000 on Facebook advertising**.

After the campaign, the company generates **৳100,000 in profit**.

**ROI = (৳100,000 ÷ ৳50,000) × 100 = 200%**

👉 The investment generated a **200% return**.

This helps the company understand whether the advertising campaign was worth the money.

---

## 📌 Why is ROI Analysis Important?

ROI Analysis helps businesses:

✅ Compare different investments
✅ Understand whether a project is profitable
✅ Make better investment decisions
✅ Decide where to spend money
✅ Measure the success of marketing campaigns
✅ Compare the performance of different projects

For example, if:

📱 Campaign A = 50% ROI
💻 Campaign B = 150% ROI

The business may consider investing more in **Campaign B**.

---

## 📌 ROI vs Cost-Benefit Analysis

These two methods are related, but they are not exactly the same.

### 💰 Cost-Benefit Analysis

Asks:

👉 **Are the total benefits greater than the total costs?**

### 📈 ROI Analysis

Asks:

👉 **How much return did we receive compared to our investment?**

### Simple Example:

You invest **৳10,000**.

You receive **৳15,000**.

**Cost-Benefit Analysis:**
The benefit is greater than the cost.

**ROI Analysis:**
Your return is **50%**.

---

📌 **In simple words:**

ROI Analysis helps answer one important question:

👉 **"If we invest this much money, how much return will we get?"**

As a Business Analyst, understanding ROI helps you evaluate whether a project, product, marketing campaign, or business decision is financially valuable.

💡 **Don't only ask, "How much will this cost?"**

Also ask:

👉 **"What will we get in return?"**

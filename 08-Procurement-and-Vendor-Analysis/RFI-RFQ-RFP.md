# 📄 RFI, RFQ & RFP: Understanding How Businesses Ask for Information and Offers

Imagine you want to buy a **new software system** for your company. 💻

You may need to:

❓ Ask companies for information
💰 Ask for a price
📋 Ask companies to propose a complete solution

This is where **RFI, RFQ, and RFP** are used.

---

## 🔵 RFI: Request for Information

### **RFI = Request for Information**

Use an RFI when:

> **You want to learn more before making a decision.**

Imagine you want to buy a new CRM system, but you do not know what options are available.

You may ask different software companies:

* What features does your system have?
* How does your software work?
* Can it integrate with other systems?
* What type of support do you provide?
* What technologies do you use?

At this stage, you are mainly **collecting information**.

### Simple Example:

> **Company:** We need a CRM system.
> **RFI:** "Please tell us about your CRM solution and its capabilities."

📌 **Main question:**

> **"What options are available?"**

---

# 🟠 RFQ: Request for Quotation

### **RFQ = Request for Quotation**

Use an RFQ when:

> **You already know exactly what you want and want to know the price.**

### Example:

You need:

💻 100 laptops
🧠 Intel Core i7
💾 16 GB RAM
💽 512 GB SSD

You send the same requirements to different suppliers.

Then you compare their prices.

| Supplier   |   Price |
| ---------- | ------: |
| Supplier A | $80,000 |
| Supplier B | $75,000 |
| Supplier C | $78,000 |

### 📌 Main question:

> **"How much will it cost?"**

---

# 🟣 RFP: Request for Proposal

### **RFP = Request for Proposal**

Use an RFP when:

> **You have a business problem and want companies to propose a complete solution.**

For example:

> "Our company wants to build an online marketplace."

You may ask vendors to explain:

* How will you solve our problem?
* What technology will you use?
* What features will you build?
* How long will development take?
* How much will it cost?
* What support will you provide?

Different companies may propose different solutions.

### 📌 Main question:

> **"How will you solve our problem?"**

---

# 🧠 Simple Example: Building a House 🏠

Imagine you want to build a house.

### 🔵 RFI

You ask:

> "What types of houses can you build?"

You are collecting information.

---

### 🟠 RFQ

You already have the house design.

You ask:

> "How much will it cost to build this house?"

You are asking for a price.

---

### 🟣 RFP

You say:

> "I want a comfortable three-bedroom house. Please tell me how you would design and build it."

You are asking for a complete solution proposal.

---

# 📊 RFI vs RFQ vs RFP

| Document   | Main Purpose        | Main Question                   |
| ---------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| 🔵 **RFI** | Collect Information | What options are available?     |
| 🟠 **RFQ** | Get Prices          | How much will it cost?          |
| 🟣 **RFP** | Get Solutions       | How will you solve the problem? |

---

# 💻 Software Engineering Example

Imagine a company wants to create a **food delivery application**.

### 🔵 RFI

The company asks software vendors:

> "Please provide information about your food delivery software solutions."

The company wants to understand the market.

---

### 🟠 RFQ

The company already has detailed requirements:

✅ Customer app
✅ Restaurant dashboard
✅ Delivery tracking
✅ Payment system

Now it asks:

> "How much will it cost to develop these exact features?"

---

### 🟣 RFP

The company says:

> "We want to build a food delivery platform. Please propose a complete solution."

The vendors may propose:

📱 App design
💻 Technology
☁️ Cloud infrastructure
⏳ Timeline
👥 Development team
💰 Budget

---

# 👨‍💼 What Does a Business Analyst Do?

A Business Analyst may help with:

🔍 Understanding the business need
📝 Writing requirements
📋 Preparing RFI documents
💰 Defining RFQ requirements
📄 Preparing RFP documents
📊 Comparing vendor responses
🤝 Supporting vendor selection

---

# 🎯 The Easiest Way to Remember

### 🔵 RFI

**I want to learn.**

> "Tell me what you can offer."

---

### 🟠 RFQ

**I know what I want.**

> "Tell me how much it costs."

---

### 🟣 RFP

**I have a problem.**

> "Tell me how you will solve it."

---

> **RFI = Information**
> **RFQ = Price**
> **RFP = Solution**

As a Business Analyst, understanding the difference between these documents is important because choosing the right request helps a company collect the right information, compare suppliers, and select the best solution. 🚀
